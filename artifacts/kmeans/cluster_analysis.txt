2. Selection Justification
--------------------------------------------------
K=4 was selected using the discrete centered second difference elbow heuristic: C(k) = I(k-1) - 2*I(k) + I(k+1) evaluated over interior candidates k in [3, 7]. At K=4, C(4) = 189.30, representing the maximum deceleration in inertia reduction among interior candidates (C(3)=135.31, C(4)=189.30, C(5)=39.81, C(6)=133.55, C(7)=-192.76). This is a discrete elbow heuristic, not proof of a unique optimal cluster count. Across all evaluated K, the observed Silhouette scores show only modest cluster separation (0.1465 to 0.1781) and disagree with the elbow heuristic, peaking at K=8 (0.1781) versus K=4 (0.1488). K=4 is retained under the elbow heuristic to balance variance reduction against model complexity.

--------------------------------------------------
Crucial Note: Clusters represent structural groupings across the 7 feature dimensions (interaction,
exploitability, privilege, and impact attributes). They do NOT equate directly to severity classes
(Low, Medium, High, Critical). Risk ratings are predicted separately via supervised learning (KNN).
